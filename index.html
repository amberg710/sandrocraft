Master of

Dublin · Ireland

For over two decades, our Dublin forge has produced iron work that kings would envy and warriors would die for. Every piece is hand-forged, never cast — shaped by fire, hammer, and a craft passed down through generations.

We forged every weapon, every shield, every piece of iron armor that appeared on screen in Game of Thrones across all eight seasons. From Jon Snow's Longclaw to the blades of the Night's Watch — our Dublin hands shaped them all.

Now, those same techniques, that same fire, that same mastery — available to commission for your home, your office, or your collection.

The Seven Fires

From ore
to legend

I

The Consultation

Every great weapon begins with a vision. We meet, we listen, we sketch — from sketch to sacred steel.

II

The Heating

Raw iron stock is brought to 1,200°C in our Dublin forge. The first transformation begins.

III

The Shaping

Hammer meets hot iron. Each component is formed by hand, the same technique used on Game of Thrones.

IV

The Welding

Components are precision-welded. Seams ground to invisibility. The piece becomes one.

V

The Engraving

Runes, sigils, or custom designs carved into the metal by hand. Your mark for eternity.

VI

The Patina

A protective patina applied — matching the aged, battle-worn finish of iconic screen weapons.

VII

The Delivery

Custom-crated, fully insured, delivered anywhere in the realm. Your relic arrives worthy of a throne room.
