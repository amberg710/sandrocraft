For over two decades, Sandro Ferro has been shaping iron into objects of extraordinary presence. Working from his Dublin atelier, every sculpture is hand-forged at 1,200°C — never cast, never rushed.

Game of Thrones — Official Armorer

Sandro created every sword, every shield, every piece of weaponry and armour that appeared across all eight seasons. From Longclaw to the blades of the Night's Watch — our Dublin atelier forged them all.

The Process

From raw ore
to legend

I

Consultation

Every commission begins with a personal consultation — understanding your vision, space, and intent.

II

Design

Bespoke drawings and schematics, refined until every detail is agreed before the forge fires.

III

Forging

Iron reaches 1,200°C. Each component shaped by hand — the same technique used on Game of Thrones.

IV

Assembly

Components precision-welded. Seams ground to invisibility. The piece becomes a unified whole.

V

Engraving

Custom runes, sigils, or house crests carved by hand. Your mark preserved in iron forever.

VI

Finishing

Hand-applied patina — aged, refined, and sealed to last centuries.

VII

Delivery

White-glove installation or custom crating with full insurance. Delivered anywhere in the world.
