Our Story

Born in the Forge

Deep in the heart of Dublin, where the ring of hammer on iron echoes through cobblestone streets, our master craftsman has spent over a decade transforming raw iron into works of legend.

Inspired by the epic worlds of fantasy — dragons, swords, ancient runes and the eternal battle between fire and ice — every sculpture that leaves this forge is a singular, unrepeatable masterpiece.

From Game of Thrones-inspired dragons to custom office installations that command respect, each piece is hand-welded, hand-finished, and built to endure for generations.
